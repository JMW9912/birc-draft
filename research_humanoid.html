Humanoid & Avatar Robotics

Robot Embodiment

Giving physical form to transferred intelligence

These platforms form the robot-facing layer of BiRC’s Co-Embodied Intelligence research: avatar and humanoid systems that receive human action, perceive physical interaction, and support increasingly autonomous manipulation and whole-body behavior.
