Prof. Joonbum Bae

Professor, School of Mechanical Engineering, Korea University · Director, BiRC Lab

Dr. Joonbum Bae is a roboticist working on Shared Physical Intelligence across humans and robots. His research explores how human sensorimotor experience can be captured through wearable and soft interfaces, transferred to robotic bodies through teleoperation and learning, and returned to people through tactile and haptic feedback. This Co-Embodied Intelligence framework connects his broader research interests in wearable human–robot interfaces, soft robotics, tactile sensing, humanoid and avatar robotics, haptics, motion analysis, and embodied intelligence.

He is a Professor in the School of Mechanical Engineering, Korea University, and Director of the BiRC Lab. Previously, he served as a Professor at UNIST from 2012 to 2024, where he was named a Rising-star Distinguished Professor in 2018. He led Team UNIST to No. 6 worldwide and No. 1 in Asia at the $10M ANA Avatar XPRIZE, and has been named among the World’s Top 2% Scientists. He is also the CEO and founder of Feel the Same, Inc., a BiRC laboratory start-up founded in 2017.
