Soft & Wearable Interfaces

Human Embodiment

Capturing and returning physical experience

These technologies form the human-facing layer of BiRC’s Co-Embodied Intelligence research: soft and wearable systems that sense motion, contact, and physical state, and provide haptic or physical feedback without constraining natural movement.
